# Job Posted Event

## Event Name
Job Posted

## Purpose
Track when a company successfully posts a job on the PlaceMux marketplace.

## Trigger
A company successfully submits and publishes a job posting.

## Actor
Company

## Skill Threshold
Each job must have a required skill threshold between L1 and L100.

## Source
Backend job-posting event.

## Business Purpose
Measure and monitor job supply on the marketplace.
